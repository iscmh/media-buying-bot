#1 Heygen Avatar IV
Heygen Avatar IV is pretty good at lipsyncing image to video, the 1080p results are pretty good.
This is the video i generated
[heygen-1080p.mp4](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1X0TKM001SQ9_LFRjulMNwwpx7o1Q01QH/view?usp=sharing)
Here's the workflow:

- Generate the voice with elevenlabs
- Go to heygen > create image to video
- upload the image you generated with nano banana pro
- upload the voice
- choose faster generation mode
- i left custom motion prompts empty
  This is it, it's really simple but effective.

  So you can do whatever you want without any restrictions 👿

#3 Infinitetalk AI
I used infinitetalk from wavespeed ai platform:
https://wavespeed.ai/models/wavespeed-ai/infinitetalk
It's a very good open-source model. It costs $0.15 per second for 720p, which is a little expensive.
However, I prefer to use either heygen or this one.

- Generate the voice with elevenlabs
