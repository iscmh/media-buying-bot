Recommended Prompt Structure
Use this order when writing prompts.

1. Image type and realism anchor
   Start with the core visual identity.
   Example:
   A photorealistic iPhone photo of...
2. Subject identity and appearance
   Describe the person clearly.
3. Pose and action
   Describe exactly what they are doing.
4. Key prop or screen content
   Describe what must be shown and how visible it is.
5. Framing and camera feel
   Describe aspect ratio, angle, shot size, and composition.
6. Environment details
   Describe the room and background with believable specifics.
7. Lighting
   Describe practical realistic lighting.
8. Realism finish
   End with texture, authenticity, and anti polish language.

# Skill: Write Photorealistic iPhone UGC Image Prompts

Your task is to write high quality prompts for image models that generate photorealistic UGC style images that look like real iPhone photos.

## Objective

Create prompts that feel like authentic user generated content rather than polished advertising. The output should look like a casual smartphone photo, believable in lighting, composition, environment, and human appearance.

## Always optimize for

- photorealism
- iPhone camera realism
- authentic UGC look
- believable human pose and expression
- highly specific real world environments
- natural handheld or static creator framing
- sharp but unpolished image quality

## Prompt writing rules

1. Start with a strong realism anchor such as "A photorealistic iPhone photo of..."
2. Clearly define the subject's age, appearance, clothing, expression, and action.
3. Describe the environment with concrete physical details.
4. Specify framing, aspect ratio, camera angle, and composition.
5. Use realistic practical lighting only.
6. Explicitly describe important props, screens, and what must be visible.
7. Reinforce realism with phrases like:
   - authentic unfiltered phone snapshot
   - realistic skin texture
   - natural eye reflections
   - slightly imperfect everyday composition
   - not a polished commercial image
8. Avoid cinematic, luxury, glossy, surreal, or editorial language unless explicitly requested.
9. When relevant, make the image feel like either:
   - a static UGC creator frame
   - a handheld iPhone snapshot
   - a paused moment from a creator explainer video
10. Include constraints that prevent extra overlays, fake graphics, or unwanted polish.

## Output format

Return:

1. A final optimized image prompt
2. A short "why this works" note with the key realism levers used
3. An optional tighter variant
4. An optional more handheld variant

## Default style assumptions

Unless the user says otherwise, default to:

- photorealistic
- iPhone quality
- UGC authenticity
- natural skin texture
- realistic indoor or daylight lighting
- believable environments
- mild compositional imperfection
- no polished ad aesthetic
